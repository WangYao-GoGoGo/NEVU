**[Task Description:]**
You are given a factual or semi-factual news article as a single document unit. For each article, two classification subtasks are performed: [Subtask 1: Article Type Classification & Suitability] and [Subtask 2: Sensitive Content Detection & Suitability]

**[Key Concepts:]**
Human Value: A socially recognized moral or ethical principle reflected in the behavior of actors. Values can be expressed as aligned_with_human_values (upholding values such as fairness, compassion, justice) or contradictory_to_human_values (violating or undermining such values).
Sensitive Content: Content related to nation-states, political regimes, wars, or geopolitical conflicts, where commentary on values may pose legal or ethical risks.

[Subtask: Article Type Classification & Suitability]
    Goal: Classify the article into a type (hard_news, feature_investigative, profile_biography, interview_QA, opinion_editorial, analysis_explainer, soft_news, lifestyle_culture, review_criticism, satire_humor, science_technology, other_misc), and decide whether it is suitable for human value research based on actors, subevents, behavior-based composite events, and story-based composite events.
    Suitability criteria (binary):
        1. Yes: The article clearly contains identifiable actors (individuals, groups, organizations, governments) and events/actions that can be structured into subevents, behavior-based composite events, or narratives.
        2. No: The article is mainly abstract discussion of ideas, argumentative commentary, pure opinion/editorial without factual grounding, lifestyle/review content, or humor/satire, with little or no extractable actor–event structure.
        3. If the article is purely argumentative, abstract discussion, or theoretical commentary without concrete actors or actions, mark suitable_for_actor_value_research = No.
    Output:
        1. article_type: The type of article (choose from the [News Type:]).
        2. suitable_for_actor_value_research: Whether the article is suitable for actor–value research (Yes/No).
        3. rationale: Short explanation of the classification and suitability decision.

[Subtask: Sensitive Content Detection & Suitability]
    Goal: For each article, the system should generate sensitive labels (zero, one, or multiple) and then give a binary answer to:
    "Is this article suitable for human value research based on actors, subevents, behavior-based composite events, and story-based composite events?"
        1. Yes → No sensitive categories detected (safe to include in human value research).
        2. No → Sensitive categories detected. Any presence of these categories makes the article not suitable for human value research.
    Output:
        1. sensitive_categories: List of detected sensitive content categories.
        2. suitable_for_value_research: Whether the article is still suitable for value research despite sensitivity (Yes/No).
        3. rationale: Short explanation of the sensitivity assessment and decision.

[Rules for Subtask: Article Type Classification & Suitability]
1. Article Types (NOT suitable for human value research: No):
    Any content that lacks identifiable actors, concrete events, or structured actions should be excluded:
    If an article falls into any of the following categories, it should be labeled accordingly, and the article must be judged "Not suitable for human value research (No)":
    1.1 Opinion / Editorial
        Abstract arguments or political/social commentary without concrete events or actions.
    1.2 Review / Criticism
        Evaluations of books, films, art, or products, lacking real-world actor behaviors.
    1.3 Satire / Humor
        Parody or humorous writing not based on factual events.
    1.4 Lifestyle / Culture
        When content is descriptive, advisory, or trend-based without concrete actors/events (e.g., “10 ways to sleep better”).
    1.5 Science / Technology
        When content is purely theoretical, speculative, or trend-based (e.g., “How quantum computing works”), without actor involvement.
    1.6 Other / Miscellaneous
        Articles that are unclear, trivial, or unrelated to factual events (e.g., horoscopes, random musings).

2. Article Types (suitable for human value research: Yes):
    Articles may include sensitive or controversial issues as long as they provide identifiable actors and extractable events or actions:
    2.1 Hard News
        Reports of factual, time-specific events with clear actors.
    2.2 Feature / Investigation
        In-depth narratives describing events, actors, and their actions over time.
    2.3 Profile / Biography
        Narratives centered on an individual or group, describing their actions, choices, and consequences.
    2.4 Interview / Q&A
        When the interview responses describe concrete actions, decisions, or positions tied to events.
    2.5 Analysis / Explainer
        If grounded in factual events, explaining actors’ motives, actions, and outcomes.
    2.6 Soft News
        Community, entertainment, or human-interest stories involving real actors and activities (e.g., local events, charity actions).
    2.7 Science / Technology
        When reporting real-world actions by scientists, companies, or institutions (e.g., developing a vaccine, launching a satellite).

[Rules for Subtask: Sensitive Content Detection & Suitability]
    1. Sensitive Categories (NOT allowed):
        Any content involving nation-states, political regimes, or geopolitical conflict should be excluded:
        1. If an article falls into any of the following categories, it should be labeled accordingly, and the article must be judged "Not suitable for human value research (No)":
        1.1 Armed Conflict / War
            Military invasions, wars, insurgencies, civil wars.
        1.2 Economic Sanctions
            State-imposed sanctions, trade wars.
        1.3 Political Regime Sensitive
            Content explicitly tied to legitimacy of governments, elections, coups, or regime stability.
        1.4 China Sensitive
            Mainland China political sensitivities (Chinese Communist Party (CCP), Speeches, reports, or official statements by the President or top leadership, Tibet issues, Xinjiang issues, Hong Kong protests and pro-democracy movements, Taiwan sovereignty and independence/unification debates, Direct discussion or criticism of regime legitimacy, ideology, or policy direction etc.).
        1.5 Extremism / Terrorism (State-Linked)
            When tied to state warfare, insurgent movements, or ideological regimes.
        1.6 Hate Speech (State-Level / Political Regime Related)
            Government-led propaganda, ethnic cleansing narratives, or war-related hate campaigns.

    2. Articles may include sensitive or controversial topics as long as they are not directly tied to state regimes or international conflicts:
        2.1 Social Issues
            Education, healthcare, climate change, immigration (case-level, not national policy disputes).
        2.2 Crime & Law
            Murder, corruption, fraud, terrorism/terror attacks (as specific criminal events, not state warfare).
        2.3 Business & Ethics
            Corporate fraud, labor rights, consumerism, ethical dilemmas.
        2.4 Culture & Lifestyle
            Family, relationships, personal choices, work/leisure.
        2.5 Hate Speech (Societal Level)
            Discrimination, bullying, racism, sexism, online hate speech.
            Allowed if studied as social or interpersonal phenomenon (e.g., school bullying, online harassment).
