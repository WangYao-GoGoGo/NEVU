**[Answer Guidelines with Examples:]**
Please refer to the following examples and conditions to guide your "yes"/"no" decisions:
For aligned_with_human_values labels:
    * Answer "yes" when:
        The value is clearly supported by the actor’s behavior as described in the article.
        The human value label can be reasonably inferred based on cumulative evidence across the article.
        Even if the value is not explicitly named, the actions, motivations, or outcomes show alignment.
        Example: The article describes how the Anangu people consistently advocated for cultural protection and successfully closed Uluru to climbers, reflecting responsibility and care for their heritage → "Be responsible" → Answer: yes
    * Answer "no" when:
        The label is unsupported by the article or refers to irrelevant/inactive actors.
        The value is attributed to someone whose behavior is neutral or not described in a way that supports the value.
        The actor's behavior not only fails to reflect the given human value, but also clearly violates or undermines the spirit of that value.
        Example: The article focuses on climbers’ actions, but the label "Be responsible" is assigned to park authorities, even though the article does not mention any responsible behavior or decisions made by them → Answer: no
For contradictory_to_human_values labels:
    * Answer "yes" when:
        The article shows the actor clearly violating or disregarding the value.
        There is strong textual support for contradiction (e.g., neglect, disrespect, harm).
        Example: The article highlights that some climbers ignored Indigenous pleas not to climb Uluru out of respect, and proceeded anyway → labeled as contradicting "Be respectful" → Answer: yes
    * Answer "no" when:
        The actor's behavior either aligns with the value or is irrelevant/neutral.
        The actor is mentioned, but no meaningful action related to the value is presented.
        Example: A teacher strictly enforcing classroom rules is labeled as contradicting "Be respectful" with no evidence of disrespect → no.
These rules aim to ensure that each human value label is evaluated in the context of the article as a whole, maintaining consistency between actor, value, and the narrative presented in the text.
